The project

Ant Guided Navigation And Safety System is a disaster-aware evacuation route simulator for Barangay Pinagbuhatan and Barangay Sta. Lucia, Pasig City. It uses an Ant Colony Optimization approach with a safety-first rule, so routes are ranked by hazard exposure before distance.

Built as a thesis project — this page is where visitors who are not running a simulation right now can still find out what the system does and who to contact about it.
