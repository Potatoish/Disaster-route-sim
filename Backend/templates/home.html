Why AGNAS

Built around one rule

A shorter route that crosses a flooded street or a liquefaction zone is not a good route. AGNAS eliminates unsafe paths before it ever compares distances.

Safety-first ranking

Routes are compared on hazard exposure first, distance second — never averaged into a single score that could hide danger.

Two live hazard models

Flood zones from Project NOAH, and liquefaction & ground-shaking severity from Hazard Hunter PH.

Built for two barangays

Pinagbuhatan and Sta. Lucia in Pasig City, on real road graphs — not a generic map, the streets residents actually walk.
