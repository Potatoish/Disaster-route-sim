Good to know

Before you start

A few things worth knowing before you plan a route.

Walking routes only

Routes are built for pedestrians, this system don't follow one-way streets or other vehicle-only restrictions.

Built on official hazard data

Flood levels come from Project NOAH. Earthquake liquefaction and ground shaking levels come from PHIVOLCS.

Plan ahead, not during an emergency

AGNAS is built for preparation, not live alerts. When a disaster strikes, rely on your local authorities for instructions.

How it works

How our ants works for you

Simulated ants map local streets — strengthening digital signals on safe roads while fading out paths with hazards.

01

Ants explore the graph

Independent, parallel search

Digital "ants" try out many different routes across the streets, checking each one for flood or earthquake danger instead of just picking one path.

02

Pheromone reinforces safe, short trails

Safer + shorter trails strengthen

Every run, the ants leave a stronger trail on safe roads and a fading trail on risky ones — like a scent trail that only lasts on the good paths.

03

Routes are ranked safety-first

Safety before distance, always

Dangerous roads are crossed out first. Only then does AGNAS pick the shortest route from what's left.
