How it works

How our ants works for you

Simulated ants map local streets — strengthening digital signals on safe roads while fading out paths with hazards.

01

Ants explore the graph

Independent, parallel search

Simulated ants perform multiple exploratory runs across the road network. They evaluate various paths based on hazard severity (flood or earthquake) and digital pheromone levels rather than relying on a single static route.

02

Pheromone reinforces safe, short trails

Safer + shorter trails strengthen

With every simulation run, digital pheromones evaporate from hazard prone routes. Meanwhile, digital signals strengthen along paths verified to be safe and clear.

03

Routes are ranked safety-first

Safety before distance, always

Routes are never measured by distance alone. The algorithm automatically eliminates paths crossing high-hazard zones before total distance is ever compared.
