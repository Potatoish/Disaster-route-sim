About AGNAS

Project overview

AGNAS helps you plan safer walking routes in Barangay Pinagbuhatan and Barangay Sta. Lucia, Pasig City. Using official flood and earthquake hazard maps from Project NOAH and PHIVOLCS, it shows which routes avoid high-risk streets, with the safest ones listed first. Use it to plan ahead before a disaster, not during an emergency.

Architecture

A three-tier build separating the map interface from the routing computation.

ClientLeaflet, OSM tiles
ServiceFlask, OSMnx, ACO engine

Data sources

  • Flood hazard levels

    Project NOAH 25-year flood hazard map. It is a modeled flood scenario, not live flood conditions.

  • Liquefaction & ground shaking

    PHIVOLCS hazard maps on HazardHunterPH, traced by the team.

  • Road network

    OpenStreetMap contributors, under the Open Database License. Downloaded with OSMnx.

  • Walking limits in flood water

    AIDR Flood Hazard Guideline 7-3 (PDF): water past 0.5 m is unsafe for children and older adults, and past 1.2 m for anyone.

  • Body heights in the flood scene

    DOST-FNRI standard Filipino adult height (163 cm).
